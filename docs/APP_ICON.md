# Home-screen icon

Created using the built-in ImageGen tool. The final opaque RGB master is
`sketchbook-app/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/Sketchbook-1024.png`.
The other PNGs in that asset set are downscaled versions for the existing
iPad and iPhone icon slots. iOS supplies the rounded corner mask.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final iPad/iPhone home-screen app icon for Shared Sketchbook, a minimal full-screen drawing app with dark violet and lavender floating controls. Create one polished square 1024x1024 opaque app-icon artwork. Full-bleed deep midnight violet background with extremely subtle rich tonal variation. Center a bold, graceful, tactile lavender paint ribbon forming a loose open loop, with a clean warm ivory digital stylus tip at the lower right connected naturally to the end of the painted stroke. The brushstroke is the hero: substantial thickness, expressive taper, smooth sculpted paint surface, elegant silhouette, a touch of luminous lilac and restrained soft depth. A small warm peach highlight can appear in the paint. Understated premium artist-tool aesthetic, graphic and simple enough to read clearly at 40px. Large centered symbol occupying about 72 percent of the square, generous safe space at the edges. No lettering, no words, no tiny detail, no border, no extra UI, no mockup, no drop shadow outside the square, no baked-in rounded corners. Square artwork must cover every edge; iOS applies its own corner mask.
